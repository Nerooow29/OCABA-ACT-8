About R.A.N.G.E.R. Cebu Chapter


(Mission, Vision, and Why choose R.A.N.G.E.R.👇)

👇Mission👇


To protect the lives and property of the citizens of our community as a team, by responding to emergency medical incidents, fires, and natural disasters.


👇Vision👇

Be a responsible volunteer organization that is dedicated to remaining as progressive and innovative medical and fire volunteers to ensure a safe community through public education in fire prevention, Fire Suppression, and Medical Emergency Training, and Service.


👇Why choose R.A.N.G.E.R.👇

R.A.N.G.E.R. Cebu Chapter is a volunteer, Non-government and Non-profit Organization. We aim to provide basic fire fighting, basic life support and medical transport in our area of responsibility.
